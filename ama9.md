### 1. What is an artifact? 

An artifact is a piece of content created by AI, such as a document, code, diagram, or webpage, that can be viewed and edited separately.

### 2. What is Claude AI? 

Claude AI is an artificial intelligence assistant developed by Anthropic that can answer questions, write content, analyze data, and generate code.

### 3. What is an LLM? 

LLM stands for Large Language Model. It is an AI model trained on large amounts of text to understand and generate human-like language.

### 4. What is AI? 

AI stands for Artificial Intelligence. It is a technology that enables computers to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence, such as learning, reasoning, and problem-solving.

### 5. What is the difference between Google Search and Claude? 

Google Search helps users find information and websites on the internet, while Claude is an AI assistant that understands prompts and generates conversational answers, explanations, and content.

### 6. What is serialization? 

Serialization is the process of converting complex data, such as objects or database records, into a format like JSON so that it can be easily stored or transferred between applications.

### 7. What are connectors in Claude? 

Connectors in Claude are integrations that allow Claude to connect with external tools and services, such as Google Drive or other supported applications, to access information and perform tasks.
